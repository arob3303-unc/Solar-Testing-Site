⚠️ Net Billing Ratio Analysis

Your utility is applying asymmetric rates to solar exports vs. imports — a growing trend across U.S. states.

Export credit
—¢/kWh
Import (retail)
—¢/kWh

—

You send 1 kWh to the grid for 1 credit, but it costs 2 credits to buy that same kWh back at night — so solar homeowners are effectively losing about 50% of their energy to the utility company. That is exactly why a battery is essential under net billing (NM 2.0): store your own power and use it yourself instead of selling it back at a loss.
